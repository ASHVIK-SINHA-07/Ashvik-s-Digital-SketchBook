## 01. Project Overview

VeriLekh AI is a collaborative technology project developed in response to Smart India Hackathon 2026 problem statement SIH26018.

The project addresses the challenges associated with digitizing and validating India's traditional land records, many of which exist in handwritten registers, scanned documents, legacy PDFs, and other non-standard formats.

The central idea behind VeriLekh AI is to explore how artificial intelligence and digital technologies can make land-record management more structured, accessible, and reliable.

Instead of treating digitization as simply converting physical documents into digital files, the project approaches it as a broader process involving document understanding, information extraction, and data validation.

The goal is to reduce the difficulties associated with manually processing legacy land documentation and establish a foundation for more efficient digital land governance.

## 02. Understanding the Problem

Land records are essential for property ownership, agricultural administration, legal verification, and government land-management processes.

However, managing traditional land documentation presents several challenges.

### Existing Challenges

**1. Legacy Documentation**

Many land records are maintained in handwritten registers, scanned documents, and older formats that are difficult to process automatically.

**2. Inconsistent Data Formats**

Land records may contain variations in terminology, layouts, regional languages, and measurement units.

**3. Manual Data Entry**

Extracting information from physical documents often requires considerable human effort, making the process time-consuming.

**4. Data Inconsistencies**

Errors, missing information, spelling variations, and conflicting entries can make verification difficult.

**5. Limited Digital Interoperability**

Converting documents into digital files does not automatically make their contents structured or compatible with other land-management systems.

### The Core Problem

How can an intelligent system extract meaningful information from legacy land records, identify inconsistencies, and support reliable digital validation?

This question forms the foundation of VeriLekh AI.

## 03. Proposed Solution

VeriLekh AI explores an AI-assisted approach to land-record digitization and validation.

The proposed system can be understood through a series of processing stages:

1. **Document Input:** Accept land-record documents in supported digital formats.
2. **Document Processing:** Prepare uploaded documents for information extraction.
3. **Text Recognition:** Apply OCR techniques to identify text from scanned or image-based documents.
4. **Information Extraction:** Identify relevant land-record fields and organize extracted information.
5. **Data Validation:** Examine extracted information for inconsistencies, missing fields, and potential errors.
6. **Human Verification:** Allow authorized personnel to review uncertain or flagged information.
7. **Structured Records:** Prepare validated information for organized digital storage and further processing.

The intention is to combine automated processing with human oversight rather than relying entirely on manual data entry or unverified automated extraction.

*Note: This describes the proposed system workflow, not a claim that every stage has been implemented in the repository.*
