A liitle bit about this space

The The Digital Desk is my personal sketchbook on the web — a space where I document what I'm building, learning, and figuring out, one page at a time. It's part portfolio, part running log: motion design experiments, small dev/AI projects, and photography, built up gradually as I pick up new skills. Nothing here is polished from day one — it's meant to grow visibly, the same way I am.
